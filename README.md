# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

A ~100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired, written by a pure-stdlib Python program for the make.fun bounty **The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue**.

> Come in. Mind the— yes, the skulls. I had them polished. Again. Sit. No, not there. That chair belonged to my brother, and I... I had him exiled, you know. Twenty years ago. He sends figs every winter. I eat every last one. Anyway. The plan is simple: at midnight my ravens take the harbor lanterns, the tide does the rest, and by dawn the city kneels. Or... crawls. Kneeling takes effort. I remember when betrayal thrilled me. Now I just want the paperwork done. So. Kneel, or don't. Bring tea first. The lanterns are already lit.

Run `python3 main.py` — it writes the monologue (97 words, 531 characters) to `/output/entry.txt` and re-reads it to assert the gate constraints (1–600 characters, about 100 words).
